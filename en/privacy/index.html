Privacy

What we do with the details you give us when you join the waitlist. In short: we write to you at launch, and that’s all.

Who is responsible

The Beacon Isle Group — Dimitri Limberopoulos EI, Paris, France.

What we record

Your email address and, if you give them, the pack you’re interested in and your country. With each sign-up we also note the date, the language of the page, the version of the sign-up wording you saw, the site you came from (its domain name only) and, if present, the campaign (“utm”) tags in the page address.

Why

To write to you when we launch, and to learn which packs and countries are most interested. We never sell or rent your details, and we don’t use them for advertising.

Legal basis

Your consent, given when you sign up. You can withdraw it at any time by writing to us; we then delete your details.

How long we keep it

Up to twelve months after the launch email. If there is no launch, everything is deleted by 31 December 2027 at the latest. And straight away if you ask.

Who can see it

Only us. Sign-ups are stored in a Google Sheet, with Google acting as our processor. The site is hosted by GitHub, the fonts are served by Bunny Fonts, and the form and visit counter run on Google Apps Script: like any host, they technically receive your device’s IP address in order to show the page. Google and GitHub may process data in the United States, under the EU–US Data Privacy Framework or standard contractual clauses.

Cookies and visit counts

This site sets no cookies. We count visits without any identifier: for each visit we record the date, the language, the page and the referring site, never your IP address or your device.

Your rights

You can see your data, have it corrected or deleted, restrict or object to its use, take a copy, and withdraw your consent: just write to us. You can also complain to the CNIL, the French data protection authority (cnil.fr).
